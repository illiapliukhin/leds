# Latest changes

## Mono split boards

- Added a black-and-white split architecture next to the existing RGB wearables. The RGB 20×20 and 28×28 boards are untouched.
- One electronics board now drives two LED-only panels through two 40-pin 0.50 mm FFC receptacles. The shared driver set is 32 rows × 32 columns, so the high-resolution panel is 32×32.
- Front of each LED panel is only the matrix and margins. Rear of each LED panel holds `J_ROW` and `J_COL`.
- First-pass outlines stay large: 136.3 mm and 166.7 mm panels, 190 × 148 mm electronics, 2.54 mm pitch, 44 mm panel margin for a non-crossing 32-net FFC fan-in.
- Every mono LED stays independently addressable. Rows, columns, and the `LED_4V1` anode rail are routed. BMI270 `IMU_SDA`, `IMU_SCL`, and `IMU_INT1` reach their reserve pads. `GND`, `VBUS`, `AON_3V3`, both `REXT` pins, `LED_LOGIC_3V3`, and `LED_OE_N` are routed. MBI5124 serial pins follow the datasheet configuration: `LED_CLK`, `LED_SDI`, `LED_LE` reach their reserve pads, and `LED_SDO` chains the first driver into the second. Both 74HC154 decoders now select the 32 row gates: `ROW_nn_Y` drives the series resistor, `ROW_nn_GATE` ties the AO3403 gate and its pull-up, `SN74LV125A` now splits that address: the reserve pads stay on `ROW_A0`–`ROW_A3`, `DEC_A_EN_N`, and `DEC_B_EN_N`, and the decoder pins move to the matching `_4V` nets. `ROW_XLAT_OE_N`, translator `VCCA`/`DIR`, and `VCCB` are routed. Address lines have 100 kΩ pulldowns to `GND`, decoder enables have 47 kΩ pull-ups to `LED_4V1`, and `ROW_XLAT_OE_N` has a 10 kΩ pull-up to `AON_3V3`. `SN74LV125A` now buffers `LED_CLK`, `LED_SDI`, `LED_LE`, and `LED_OE_N`: the reserve pads stay on the MCU names, and the MBI5124 pins move to `LED_CLK_Y`, `LED_SDI_Y`, `LED_LE_Y`, and `LED_OE_Y`. `LED_CLK`, `LED_SDI`, and `LED_LE` have 100 kΩ pulldowns. `LED_OE_N` has a 47 kΩ pull-up to `AON_3V3`. The three buffered edges pass through 22 Ω, and `LED_OE_Y` has a 47 kΩ pull-up to `LED_LOGIC_3V3`. `TPS63802` now feeds `LED_4V1` from `SYS` with the 681 kΩ / 91 kΩ divider, `TPS7A2033` holds `AON_3V3`, and the LED `TPS22917` feeds `LED_LOGIC_3V3`. `LED_EN` and `LED_LOGIC_EN` have 100 kΩ pulldowns. The buck now has 10 µF on `SYS` and 2×22 µF on `LED_4V1`, and the always-on LDO has 1 µF on input and output. `BQ25185` is now the 10-pin DLH package, and its exposed pad is the datasheet 0.90 × 1.50 mm land. `VBUS`, `SYS`, and `BAT_RAW` each have a local 0805 10 µF capacitor. For a 1000 mAh cell the charger is set to 4.2 V and 500 mA: `ILIM/VSET` is 18 kΩ and `ISET` is 600 Ω. `J_BAT` is Molex PicoBlade 53398-0371 and mates with Jauch LP523450JU (No. 249796, housing 51021-0300). Pin 1 is `BAT_RAW`, pin 2 is `TS_MR`, and pin 3 is `GND`. The header is turned so black is the top pin and red is the bottom pin, matching the pack drawing. The pack NTC is 10 kΩ β3435, so no onboard resistor sits in parallel on `TS/MR`. ESP32-S3 sits on the top edge, with the crystal under it and BMI270 on that same edge. The open strip to the right is the MCU pin-escape field.
